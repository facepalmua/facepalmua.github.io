Privacy policy

JARVIS is a personal tool with one user: its owner, Bogdan Zakharchyshyn. This policy covers the data it reads from his own Google accounts.

What JARVIS accesses

Google serviceAccessWhat is read
Google CalendarRead-onlyEvents: title, time, attendees, organiser, location and description.
GmailRead-onlyMessage metadata: sender, subject, date and labels. Message bodies are not requested or stored.
Google accountEmail addressTo tell the owner's two accounts apart.

JARVIS cannot send email, change or delete messages, or create, edit or delete calendar events.

How it is used

Only to show the owner his own schedule and inbox and to answer questions he asks about them, such as "what meetings do I have today?".

Where it is stored

In a database on the owner's own computer. It is not uploaded to any server that JARVIS controls — JARVIS has none. Backups of that database are encrypted. Access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, with one exception. When the owner asks JARVIS a question, the relevant part of his own data may be sent to Anthropic's Claude to write the answer, under his own Anthropic account. It is used only to answer that question.

Data from Google is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

JARVIS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all further reads. Deleting the local database removes everything JARVIS has stored.
