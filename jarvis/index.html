A private assistant that lives on one person's computer.

JARVIS brings together its owner's calendar, meeting notes, tasks and messages so they can be searched and asked about in one place. It has exactly one user, and it runs on that user's own Mac.

Not a public service. There is no sign-up and nothing to install. The Google sign-in for JARVIS is used only by its owner, to connect his own accounts.

What it does with Google

  • Google Calendar (read-only): reads upcoming and recent events so the owner can see his day and ask about his meetings.
  • Gmail (read-only): reads message headers — sender, subject, date — to show what needs a reply. It never reads message bodies, and never sends, deletes or changes anything.

How that data is handled is in the privacy policy.
